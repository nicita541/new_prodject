# 0055 — Native qualification at the candidate story boundary

Status: implementation in progress; standalone remains opt-in.

## Evidence and decision

The controlled120s standalone trial produced0 ready stories despite50
format-valid resolved native decisions. Legacy whole-region confidence gates
rejected every region; dense views were often only1–3s and could split speech
or transfer actions between neighboring units. Stronger temporal proof improves
ownership checks but does not establish a complete standalone story.

Replace that mandatory chain for new standalone analyses with a versioned
candidate contract. Keep the full sparse source survey and episode map as
hypotheses. Build stable physical intervals preserving full ASR utterances and
natural shots; they make no semantic-scene claim. A source-backed proposal
chooses ordered groups of original intervals. An independent native review
sees the actual proposed timeline plus original omitted context, not the
proposer's explanation. Require grounded opening/development/ending,
complete retained dialogue coverage, causal/context closure, suitability for
Shorts and explicit audio/action boundary checks. Original quotes and frame
ownership are validated mechanically. Self-reported scalar confidence does
not become a calibrated probability or a legacy .9 field.

This replaces micro-unit selection plus inherited text-only structure/dialogue
passes in this objective, rather than adding another mandatory pass over them.
These questions can change the candidate; none surveys unrelated
episode material in depth. Unknown or context-limited stories become separate
review drafts. Technical failures stay distinct and do not veto independent
qualified stories. No forced count or fabricated intermediate proof is allowed.

## Ownership, validation and compatibility

Preserve physical source/ASR/frame artifacts and old raw answers. Use the
existing StageJournal and useful-question reservation for exact source/model/
schema/evidence reuse, including known negative outcomes. Check cache before
model acquisition. No new service, model, dependency or money ledger is added.
Source-only discovery has its own stage signature, excluding candidate and
legacy selection code. Candidate questions bind the exact prompt/schema/images
and adapter identity; deterministic compiler changes revalidate saved answers.
After a proposal, extend physical context around its actual audio handles and
neighboring original intervals; this does not add them to the selected story.
No repeated enrichment question is sent without genuinely new owned images.
The new pure quality validator revalidates native candidate evidence and binds
it to the exact deterministic cuts; it never relabels an unresolved legacy
analysis as approved. Existing word/timeline/micro-edit validation, final
framing and full-decoded immutable publication remain mandatory.

Old selective analyses and the legacy episode objective keep their own
validators. Rollback retains their readers and previously committed media.
Real source/cold-warm/independent editorial acceptance remains required; authored
tests or an approved model verdict alone are not release acceptance.

## Cut-only audience check and bounded correction

Real source-aware approvals did not establish audience comprehension. A fresh
120s full trial rendered two native-approved clips, but independent examination
of their output frames/subtitles found contextual openings and unfinished
endings. Source context can explain an omission that a new viewer cannot see.

`candidate_screen` therefore projects only the actual output timeline, retained
exact ASR and retained original images. Original timestamps, omitted context,
source interval names, hypotheses and previous verdicts are excluded from the
model payload. Its private binding owns source SHA, exact geometry, projected
text and image ownership. Both the source review and this cut-only verdict must
pass the pure publication validator. A model approval remains weaker evidence
than independent editorial evaluation, especially without listening to audio.

A valid negative viewer verdict may identify one concrete payoff already present
and propose a trim using opaque retained interval IDs. Translate it only into a
strict chronological subset of the current semantic core, preserve existing
source holes, then check the rebuilt audible cuts remain inside the parent edit.
Run fresh source and cut-only checks of that changed edit once. Preserve the
original proposal/verdict as history; neither that history nor the repair reason
enters the independent checks. No repeated shortening loop or approval from
omitted source context is allowed. A still-unresolved result stays a draft.

Real controlled requalification exposed an ambiguous blocking-issue contract:
the source reviewer marked all criteria true while listing optional context
suggestions and explicitly saying the mini-story remained understandable.
Source and viewer schemas now accept optional `notes` for such observations;
their prompts judge the local story/payoff rather than requiring resolution of
the whole episode's conflict or every character's biography. Blocking `issues`,
failed criteria, actual causes/answers/actions and ending proofs retain their
original authority. Historical issues are never reclassified automatically;
changed prompts/schemas require new qualification, not modified cached answers.
