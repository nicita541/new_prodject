## Source structure and continuity

`story_structure_pass` derives canonical entities and before/after facts, supported unit roles, cause/response/state relations and source-scoped arcs from accepted internally generated visual evidence and ordinary ASR. It never receives the preferred part count. Multiple arcs can belong to one parent scene. Exact source proof IDs and scoped event/unit references constrain every supported assignment; missing facts remain unknown. A closed arc requires an actual supported cause/resolution or question/response.

`dialogue_relations` examines each CURRENT source unit and exact ASR quotes without requiring punctuation. Confirmed required exchanges protect both endpoints, including merged/split speech and questions whose answer is in a different parent. Both omitted endpoints do not automatically force their restoration. Missing CURRENT coverage remains unresolved.

`source_continuity` evaluates the actual retained source intervals. It restores complete known positive units when required, including omissions inside a retained parent. Unverified three-second endpoint restoration has been removed. Canonical state gaps that remain unknown route to `continuity_verification`, which reopens original omitted footage and actual endpoint frames. Accepted source inspections carry image hashes, checkpoints, exact gap geometry and known unit references.

The dramatic planner costs inferred arc ranges rather than their complete shared parent. The existing finalized episode profile and mandatory/optional budget remain authoritative. Accepted arc endings inherit their source relation proofs. Physical actions and required dialogue cannot be split at part boundaries.

## Pre-render quality

`final_quality` runs before planning and again on fresh or cached plans before export. It checks source selection and per-unit structure/dialogue coverage, source proof for every retained interval, word-safe boundaries, full retained actions and required exchanges, bridge evidence, actual continuity and unverified boundaries inside inferred arcs. Removing source obligations from derived plan metadata cannot bypass these checks.

The pipeline performs at most three automatic recovery passes. A persistent unresolved result raises `analysis_unresolved` before export and saves the failed audit. Changed semantic scene geometry rebuilds source units and relations within the already owned provider session. The evidence chain records source unit/frame proof, semantic understanding, relations, selection reasons, continuity restoration and part-ending reasons.
